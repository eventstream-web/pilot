Our Services

Modular, scalable services that can be combined into tailored packages for your event type and budget.

1

Event Planning & Management

For: MSMEs, associations, training providers, and organizers
  • Event concept and format design
  • Budgeting and timeline management
  • Venue sourcing and logistics coordination
  • Supplier and vendor management
  • Program flow, run-of-show, and speaker coordination
  • On-site event management and operations
Outcome: A smoothly run event that aligns with your brand and business goals.
2

Live Streaming & Hybrid Event Production

For: Product launches, conferences, seminars, workshops, training, and expos
  • Multi-camera setup with professional switching
  • Low-latency streaming to your chosen platforms
  • Graphics, overlays, lower-thirds, and branding
  • Integration with virtual event platforms or custom landing pages
  • Hybrid session design (in-person + online participation)
  • On-demand recording and content archiving
Outcome: A broadcast-quality event that reaches both your physical and digital audiences reliably.
3

Interactive Attendee Engagement

For: Training programs, seminars, workshops, and conferences
  • Live polling and real-time Q&A
  • Chat moderation and audience interaction tools
  • Networking lounges and breakout rooms (virtual/hybrid)
  • Gamification elements (quizzes, challenges, leaderboards)
  • Session feedback and satisfaction surveys
Outcome: Higher engagement, better learning outcomes, and more meaningful connections among attendees.
4

Post-Event Analytics & Reporting

For: All clients who want measurable results
  • Attendance and viewership metrics (live and on-demand)
  • Session-by-session engagement analysis
  • Poll and Q&A participation data
  • Lead generation and exhibitor performance (for expos)
  • Sponsor visibility and interaction reports
  • Actionable recommendations for future events
Outcome: Clear insights that show ROI, highlight strengths, and guide improvements for your next event.
5

Expo & Tradeshow Solutions

For: Exhibit and expo organizers, industry associations, and trade groups
  • Event master planning and floor layout coordination
  • Exhibitor management and booth coordination
  • Keynote and panel live streaming
  • Virtual exhibitor booths and digital catalogs
  • AI-assisted buyer-seller matchmaking (optional)
  • Exhibitor and sponsor analytics dashboards
Outcome: A modern tradeshow that serves both on-site visitors and a wider digital audience, with measurable value for sponsors and exhibitors.
