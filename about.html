About EventStream

Closing the gap between traditional event planning and digital event innovation.

Our Story

Built for organizers who want more than a well-run day

We are a modern Event Management & Technology company founded to close the gap between traditional event planning and digital event innovation. Our team brings together event producers, technical directors, creative strategists, and data analysts who share one goal: to make every event more impactful, more accessible, and more measurable.

We work with MSMEs that need professional execution and smart technology, as well as event and training organizers who want to integrate live streaming and analytics without managing multiple vendors.

Vision

To be the trusted partner for organizations that want events that perform—live, online, and beyond the event day.

Mission

To deliver seamlessly executed events enhanced by modern technology, so our clients can focus on their message while we handle the experience, the broadcast, and the data.
